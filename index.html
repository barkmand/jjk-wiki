"Hokuna" is Sukuna in Honoka's body. She was taken over at the end of the Culling Games arc and is replaced by Sukuna's true form during/after the Gojo VS Sukuna fight.

Contents

    1. Appearance 2. Abilities and Powers 2.1. Shrine 2.2. Perfection 3. History 3.1. VS Hanako 3.2. VS Gojo

Appearance

He has Honoka's body, but with his tattoos and significantly more confidence.

He has all the hair slicked back to avoid getting anything in his eyes.

He wears a white gi with the sleeves cut off, a black belt, and a black shirt underneath.

His shoes are the same type as Naoya's.

Abilities and Powers

Shrine

Sukuna can send invisible slashes flying without any indicators, this is called Dismantle.

He can significantly empower these slashes by touching the target, this is called Cleave.

After "heating up the furnace" by fighting for a while, he can create an incredibly hot fire arrow that requires charge time and is easy to dodge, this is called Furnace.

He is one of two characters in the series with an open domain. His domain imbues both kinds of slashes into the barrier, and if he uses Furnace, the entire area lights up in flame.

Perfection

Sukuna can perfect his movements for a short period after hitting a target, although this only gives him slightly better reaction time as all his movements are already perfect.

During the prep time before the final battle, Sukuna worked with Kenjaku, another person with multiple techniques, to learn to switch between the imbued technique in his domain on will, although this exhausts both techniques after the domain breaks.

While under the effects of Perfection's sure-hit domain effect, Sukuna can achieve impossible feats, like mimicking the atomic-level cursed energy control of Gojo's six-eyes.

Using that, he manipulates the very fabric of his cursed energy to mimic a cursed energy trait that he has seen: Fire. He can only do this while under Perfection's sure-hit domain effect.

History

Sukuna takes over Honoka's body shortly after her awakening. He takes it to the Bath, then goes to kill Hanako.

VS Hanako

Sukuna fights Hanako using only Perfection to make Honoka feel like her mother died at her hand. Hanako puts up a good fight, endlessly reincarnating and building up an extreme amount of heat, and with a bit of help from Honoka resisting control, Sukuna is almost pushed to use his Shrine, but he ultimately beats her with Perfection's domain.
